**Captain's Blog, stardate -296463.3042237442.**
Today is the day that I begin posting to the Captain's Blog. It will surely be an endeavor to maintain a consistent schedule throughout the busy ups-and-downs of life aboard the *WWW-Internet,* however, I am confident that I am locquacious enough to keep good company and a font of ideas flowing. 

Though my work may be, at times, quite lengthy, they are not works of academic quality, nor should be considered as such. They are merely the opinions of a young captain navigating the starry seas of the post-modern pop-media landscape. 

Should the good people of the Web Galaxy find my work interesting, perhaps it will allow me some opportunities in the future. 

Of course, I am rather apprehensive of their reception...
