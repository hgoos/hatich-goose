The voice that lives inside my head 
is kindest least to me
Of sorts inverted Nightingale
she leaves me there to bleed
So weak I lie in gules smeared
by self-inflicted blow
I beg her please to stitch me up
and staunch the bitter flow
Or if she won’t apply to me
triage for soldier-downed
Then may she give me final rites 
and put me in the ground
<span style="margin-left:2.5em;">Much rather I my brains and lungs all stuffed inside a jar,</span> 
<span style="margin-left:2.5em;">Then let her venom patch me wrong and leave another scar.</span>
