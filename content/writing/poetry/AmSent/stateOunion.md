**American Woman / 미국 여자**
*March 16th, 2026*
I am still waiting to become a beautiful woman all grown up.

---
**Material Girl**
*March 24th, 2026*
Americans are a synthetic people made of hopes and dreams.

---
**Pedagogy of an American**
*April 7th, 2026*
*For my Uncle Herrick*
He taught me the difference between *Endo-* and *eto-* mology.

___
**Pompeii**
*April 25th, 2026*
Astounding acts of kindness—the ash doused mother and child shared no kin.

___
**Sheetless Ship, American Mouth**
*June 13th, 2026*
The British brogue fermented in the South amongst the bogs and gators. 

___
***Copa Mundial* or The North American Bid**
*June 28th, 2026*
When we can no longer hold Christmas in No-Man’s-Land; then we are lost. 

___
**American Brambles / Himalayan Blackberry**
*July 26th, 2026*
Nothing like the sudden smell of blackberries off a gravel bike route.

___
**American Minds**
*June 28th, 2026*
“We the People [are…]" misologists, misoneists, mephistophelean.

___
**American Gullet**
*August 13th, 2026*
Out here you remember how easily the land could just swallow you whole.
