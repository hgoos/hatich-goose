Hey darlin’ meet me when the sun goes down
Bring yourself and a pack of Newport Blues
The shadow rising at the edge of town

They said this place gonna’ be hallowed ground
Now we don’t got the money to pay Union dues
Hey darlin’ meet me when the sun goes down

Hop in the car and hear that blessed sound
Three years and no raises and no prop goes through
The shadow rising at the edge of town

So me and the boys, we robbed a motown
Thought we’d made off but we’re all on the news
Hey darlin’ meet me when the sun goes down 

I don’t got much time ‘til I’m in the ground
When I tell you I love you, know that it’s true
The shadow rising at the edge of town. 

If I gotta’ go, then in your arms I’ll drown
Of those that will miss me I’ve got so few
So bury me out where the sun goes down
Away from the shadows at the edge of town.
