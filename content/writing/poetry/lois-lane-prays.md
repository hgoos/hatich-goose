*"Christ, Clark,"* Lois says,
She’s holding her head in her hands
She’s watching the news unsurprised 
Rage in her tears and her eyes
The wine glass half empty, the notebook untouched
None of her words seem to matter enough. 

Jimmy is boots on the ground 
Lois is grounded for fear of the sound 
Of her voice and she’s home and she’s sick 
Watching the news unable to flick
Away from the channels 
With smoke in the air 
She closes her eyes and sends up a prayer

She prays for the principles, rights, and the laws
She prays for those shouting and bringing it all
She prays for the dreamers with hope on their tongues 
She prays for the men in the masks with the guns
She prays for Perry and Jimmy and Cat 
She prays that they’ll all make it safe back
She prays for her husband, loving and kind
She prays for the hurt that she sees in his eyes
The weight of the world---to make it all new
It’s something that only  her husband can do

The news camera swings to a shot of the sky 
A red and blue dot suspended on high
He says not a word as he touches the ground
He settles unscathed in the chaos around
He catches a bullet, a can, on his head
The close up is fuzzy with pixels and noise
He looks to the people who have but one choice

Lois can see that he’s holding it back
The rage and the pain for the courage he lacks 
To take it up top, to end it all now,
To make them all bend to his will on the ground. 

He turns to the people who watch him in awe
A man above men, a hero—a god;
A crack of a gun, a scream from the masses
A motion blurred snip of the men in the masks
The man in the blue and the red with the cape
Turns to the men and their hands as they quake
He’s caught the rouge bullet, it’s held in his palm
She knows that he fears that it’s all going wrong
She sees his lips moving but can’t hear the sound, 
He’s speaking to one man, *“Please son, put the gun down.”*

He’s reaching a hand out to pickets and signs
He’s reaching a hand across enemy lines
He’s helping a young person up from their knees
She can’t hear the words but she knows that he pleads
With tears in his eyes, with the love in his cup 
*“There’s nobody else,”* he’s saying, *“it’s only us.”*
