Poetry September 29, 2025

The Sonnets of Keris

Keris is a poet. He is well known for his role in the folktale Alisandre of the Lake, with whom he was in love with. Keris, Alisandre, and Corrinthia of the Bears were deeply embroiled in each other’s lives, living together as lovers. Below is a ballad describing a version of the tale.

Keris’ Sonnets (or Kerisian Sonnets, as they are known) are comprised of three quatrains followed by a couplet. The four lines of the quatrain alternate iambic tetrameter and iambic trimeter, respectively. The couplet is a fourteener (iambic heptameter). The rhyme scheme is ABAB CDCD EFEF GG.

Poetry March 27, 2026

Sticks and Stones

I wield my weapons like my words
As sharp as scalpel’s edge
For when I speak or write or think
I have no fear of hedge.
People seem to think that there’s
No skill in language gained
That since you speak and write and think
No growth can be exchanged.
For when you write or read so well
It’s awe that you might hear
For folks don’t seem to grasp the truth
Of learning without fear.
For just as iron horses ride by complex gears and heat
So too to wield your tongue like knives is its own grand feat.

Poetry March 24, 2026

The Body of an American / Death Wish

Lord I pray that when I die
It’s peaceful as it goes
That I should leave sans pain or hurt
And cast off earthly woes
My mother’s father’s wake was short
and but few did appear
But more akin to Psyche than
Calypso were their tears.
No Faunus at the casket’s close
No wine or toasts were raised
No comfort found in jubilee
but relief in Charon’s shade.
When comes my turn to cross the Styx and leave these banks behind
Let those who weep weep not with joy nor remember me unkind.

Faith Sonnet

I’s told when I was young.
The older I have weatherd
the truer it’s become.

Burst forth from hardship, toil, and pain and make just like a

Mummification at Constantinople

The voice that lives inside my head
is kindest least to me
Of sorts inverted Nightingale
she leaves me there to bleed
So weak I lie in gules smeared
by self-inflicted blow
I beg her please to stitch me up
and staunch the bitter flow
Or if she won’t apply to me
triage for soldier-downed
Then may she give me final rites
and put me in the ground
Much rather I my brains and lungs all stuffed inside a jar,
Then let her venom patch me wrong and leave another scar.

Poetry January 29, 2025

Heliophile

I feel each pulse inside me,
a drawing towards the light,
And steady goes my beating heart
Since end of longest night.
Each day is like a blessing
The twilight lingers just
A little more with passing days;
I revel in the dusk.
My mind has been aflame with words
And fervent they doth pour
Out of my mouth onto the page
Since winters’ losing score.
And so I write with cogent blaze and trail the sun in kind,
God, I think, it’s such a gift to even be alive.

Poetry January 13, 2025

A Poker Game with the Anemoi

At card games I have never been
a deft or winning hand
I lack the military mind
that’s built for cards and land.
But still I fumble through the play
for neither Spring nor Fall
Nor Summer have a better mind
to win from me at all.
But Winter she’s a tactful match
and always calls my bluff
And steals my winnings like a sponge
of darker matter stuff.
But as the days go on and on, the winner’s hand goes slack
And bit by bit come longest night my daylight I win back.

Summer Folio, Sonnet 13

(Commonly known as the Music Sonnet)

When first I came unto this land
across the stormy sea
I brought my binjar and my songs
and all my harmonies.
I wandered long, a troubadour,
in search of newer sound
And in this land so large and vast
a freshness did abound.
And lo! It had me in a hold
of scales and notes so fine,
I could not help but copy-straight
so I could make it mine.
And so I pluck my strings with care and hope that you will hear
The echoes of my homeland and the love that I found here.

Untitled Lake Sonnet / Gray Matter Sonnet

The brume and mist they hang about
My lake and frontal lobe
The sun it breaks through far too late
To brighten my abode.
Tempestuous, the clouds droop low
and lap up at the waves
Amygdala, like cloud-stirred lake
Refuses to behave.
Hippocampus, she rebels
And she unlike the lake
Has no reason to upheave
Like wind or snow or slake.
And though I have all everything that anyone could want
I find my mind is like the lake in deep gray winter months.

My Lady of the Lake --- Ode of the 520

The Ocean is a fickle Love
of salt and wind and brine
But she is next to nothing here
against this mistress mine.
My Lady she is of the lake
and mist and rain and fog
She one moment to the next
is sullen, angry, calm.
She blows no storms nor winds of strength
nor brings torrential rains
But batter she this concrete bridge
on which my love sustains.
My Lady Lake I fear her not on days when it is clear
But when she rages capped in white I know not to draw near.

Winter Folio, Sonnet 1

(Commonly known as Keris’ Grief Begins)
In azure vault my lady lies,
(she thinks I do not know)
She hangs suspended like a cloud,
in a frigid sky below.
If like a stage-hand I could draw
that fractal glass aback,
I would soon soar in frozen sky
and win my lady back.
See her dance among the stars
of winter there below?
She like a falcon dips and weaves,
among celestial snow.
But hollow ice and hollow hearts with weight begin to crack,
And in this hollow chamber here, I know she shan’t come back.
