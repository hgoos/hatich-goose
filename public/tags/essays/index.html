Sanctuary

The image in this work is in reference to Heroes in Crisis #2, written by Tom King and illustrated by Clay Mann.



In 1938, the dream of two Jewish boys came to fruition in the brightly colored, hot off the presses, Action Comics #1.

When I was 6 years old, I first read the Chronicles of Narnia. It was clear to me, even as a child what the story was about. “Aslan is just Jesus,” I told my father, who seemed surprised but pleased that I’d caught on so quickly, “he’s like God.” Maybe this is where it started.
