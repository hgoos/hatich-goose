A Glossary of Terms

July 14, 2026

Welcome to the Captain’s Blog Glossary of Terms. Until the Captain gets off her ass and actually modifies this page to be a proper glossary, you can use Ctrl + F in order to search for terms. Terms are organized alphbetically and will be added on an as-needed basis. Apologies for the inconvinience.

Erotica: A sub-genre of the Romance Genre. These works are primarily concerned with telling stories of an erotic or pornographic nature, however, the emphasis on the story itself may be just as important as the soft-core (or sometimes hard-core) porn.

Genre Fiction: Fiction written in a specific style of literary fiction. Genre fiction often includes tropes of the genre that fans of said genre would be familiar with. (i.e., elves and dwarves in High Fantasy, corporate monopoly and transhumanism in Cyberpunk).

Military Science Fiction: Science fiction set within the military or with a special interest in the miltary and its technolgical developments. Examples include Ender’s Game, Battlestar Galactica, and Starship Troopers.

Romance / Romance Fiction: A genre of fiction primarily focused on the interpersonal romantic relationships between people. This is an umbrella term that can encapsulate everything from Supernatural Romance (think: Twilight) to Erotica (think: 50 Shades of Gray).

Xenofiction: Stories focused on the non-human perspective, especially that of animals, concerned with telling the story through their eyes. This genre commonly overlaps with children’s literature and adventure fiction. Famous examples include Watership Down and Wind in the Willows.
