Panic / Pandemic / Pandæmonium

I had no care when I was young
But folly that was mine
For strong my dreams of hope and love
Were for all mankind.
I used to dream of green and blue
And soared through sunset clouds
But words and scrolls from far off lands
Became my nightly shroud.
Now when I dream I dream of flames
And trekking o’er dead lands
And mourning all the things that were
Or never got to have.
And since that time when time stood still (though not the rising dead),
I have been filled with terror deep; its truest name is dread.

Trivia /Heimdall /Èṣù---The Many-Faced God

When I was young, oh how the world
Seemed sprawled afore my eyes!
That steepest plunge to foreign lands
Would ever be my prize.
But as I grew so too my fear
This clenching in my core
Til’ phantom pain anxiety
Would haunt me evermore.
Then came my flight from restful nest
(Though so not far I flew)
And nothing but the here and now
Was real when I’s with you.
But soon I think I’ll find Elegua just outside my door,
And if my fear is shrunk or waits I’ll know forevermore.
